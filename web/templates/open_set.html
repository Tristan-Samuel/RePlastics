How to read the four distances

Each mode asks a different question. Compare a photo only with the mark for that same mode. A Mahalanobis of 7 is not a worse cloud distance than 0.4.

Cloud · nearest centroid
Cosine distance to the closer of the metal and plastic centroids. Use it when the question is “does this look like either class at all?” Cardboard far from both centroids scores high and becomes others. An odd bottle that still sits near the plastic centroid scores low and stays. A high value means outside both clouds.
Call · centroid of the winning class
Cosine distance to the centroid of the class the score picked, not the nearer one. Use it when a confident call might still be a strange example of that class. Plastic can win while the vector sits far from the plastic centroid, if metal is even farther. A high value means that call is atypical.
k-NN · 10 nearest train photos
Mean cosine distance to the 10 closest training photos. Use it when a shape the centroid averages away should still be rejected. An empty belt or a sponge can land near the middle of a wide class and look fine to the centroid, while no training photo actually looks like it. A high value means nothing in train is close.
Mahalanobis · 32-d, one covariance
Distance after the train vectors are reduced to 32 components, with one covariance shared by both classes. Use it when a class cloud is a long ellipse and cosine treats the long direction like the short one. A photo can be close in cosine and still sit outside the dense part of the cloud. The number is not a cosine. Read it only against this mode’s own mark. A mark near the top of the plot means this mode is rejecting almost nothing, so the keep line will not move the tiles until the mark comes down.

The dashed vertical line is the confidence hold. Left of it, a photo still inside the cloud is uncertain. The dashed horizontal line is the distance mark. Above it, the photo is others. The solid vertical line is the keep rule, and it is drawn only while that box is on. Right of it, the winning class is kept even above the distance mark. Validation 99% is the distance of a correct validation photo. It is not a percent of photos rejected. Test did not choose it. Fit searches validation only, and it skips a candidate that would hold more than 5% of that split.

Newly others

The confidence hold would accept these. The keep line does not save them, so distance, or disagreement, marks them others.

Kept anyway

Past the distance mark, or closer to the other class, but confidence is on the keep line so the winning class stays.
